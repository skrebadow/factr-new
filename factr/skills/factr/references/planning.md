# Planning dependencies and group risk

## Identify the planning relation

- Behavioral prerequisite: a result or condition needed for another outcome.
- Validation need: access, data, an observation point or correlation needed to assess an outcome.
- Planning decision: a scope or alternative choice that changes what the plan addresses.
- Execution or scheduling constraint: a sequence or resource constraint affecting the validation plan.

For each relation, identify the affected behavior and the practical implication for the plan. A common component, shared setup or adjacent source position alone does not establish required ordering. An existing measurement is not itself a dependency.

One shared planning need can serve several commitments; keep distinct needs where their planning implications differ.

## Synthesize exposure

Explain the shared failure condition or dependency and the consequences for the affected behaviors. Summarize common exposure once and include protective conditions or mitigations that change it. A shared label alone does not create a group-wide failure condition.

Risk assessment can address a single behavior. Qualitative exposure does not require a numeric score.

For a rubric-based assessment, identify the factor definitions and the values used in each calculation. Keep a final score with unknown factors unresolved; conditional calculations for stated alternatives can inform the planning choice.

## Publication and audit example

Publication and audit recording may need separate observations correlated to the same submitted message. That validation relation does not establish that audit recording occurs after publication.

A supplied rule that both outcomes become unavailable when a shared authorizer is unavailable supports common exposure for those two outcomes. It does not establish exposure for an independent reporting path.
