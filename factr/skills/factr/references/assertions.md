# Assertion selection and calibration

## Commitment versus procedure

"Verify that settings survive restart" is a tester instruction. "Settings retain the configured value after restart" states the system commitment that instruction intends to challenge.

## Calibrate the implication

"May publish to an authorized topic" supplies a permission baseline. It does not specify every other topic's policy, the transport protocol or a particular rejection response.

For a quality requirement, identify the metric, population, load, percentile and observation window that define the measurement. "Fast" is not itself a 100 ms threshold, and a percentile is different from an average.
